## What Are Anxiety Disorders?
Anxiety is a normal human reaction to stress. However, in people with anxiety disorders, feelings of fear, dread, and uneasiness are not temporary and can be overwhelming. Anxiety disorders can interfere with daily activities such as job performance, schoolwork, and relationships.

## Common Signs and Symptoms
- Feeling restless, wound-up, or on edge
- Being easily fatigued
- Having difficulty concentrating or feeling like your mind goes blank
- Being irritable
- Having muscle tension
- Difficulty controlling feelings of worry
- Having sleep problems, such as difficulty falling or staying asleep, restlessness, or unsatisfying sleep
- Physical symptoms like rapid heart rate, sweating, trembling, or rapid breathing

## Treatment and Educational Coping Strategies
Anxiety disorders are generally treatable with:
- Psychotherapy, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) which teaches different ways of thinking, behaving, and reacting to anxiety-producing situations.
- Stress management techniques, mindfulness meditation, and regular physical exercise.
- Support groups and structured self-care routines.
- Medications prescribed by licensed psychiatrists or clinicians when appropriate.

## When to Seek Urgent Help
If anxiety is persistent and disrupting your ability to function, seek an appointment with a licensed mental health professional or primary care physician. If you experience sudden chest pain and shortness of breath that could indicate a medical emergency, seek emergency medical care. If you have thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life, reach out immediately to a national crisis lifeline (such as 988 in the US, 14416 / 1800-599-0019 in India, or 112/911/999).
