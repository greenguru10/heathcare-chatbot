# ICMR Guidelines: Maternal and Child Nutrition

**Source:** Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)
**URL:** https://www.icmr.gov.in/guidelines/maternal-child-nutrition.html
**Publication Date:** 2023-01-10
**Last Updated:** 2024-12-15
**Authority Tier:** A
**Category:** maternal_and_child_health

## Nutrition During Pregnancy
Proper maternal nutrition during pregnancy is critical for fetal growth, maternal health, and safe delivery:
- An additional intake of energy (approximately 350 kcal/day in 2nd and 3rd trimesters) and high-quality protein is recommended.
- Micronutrient supplementation including iron-folic acid (IFA) is recommended from the second trimester to prevent maternal anemia and neural tube defects.
- Adequate calcium and vitamin D intake supports fetal skeletal development.
- Pregnant women should consume green leafy vegetables, legumes, whole pulses, dairy products, and seasonal fruits.

## Infant Feeding and Care
- Early initiation of breastfeeding within one hour of birth (colostrum) provides essential antibodies.
- Exclusive breastfeeding is strongly advised for the first 6 months without any water, honey, or other liquids.
- Complementary feeding with locally available, hygienic, energy-dense foods should begin at 6 months alongside continued breastfeeding up to 2 years.

## Pregnancy Warning Signs
Pregnant women should be monitored by qualified obstetric care providers. Seek immediate medical care if any of the following occur:
- Severe persistent headache or blurred vision
- High blood pressure or sudden swelling of face and hands
- Vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage
- Reduced fetal movements
- Severe abdominal pain or high fever
